# Gesture theory

The theoretical literature behind co-speech gesture generation: studies of why and how people gesture when they speak, how gesture relates to speech and thought, and how it is perceived. These papers build no generation system; the practical work is in the [main list](README.md).

This list was assembled from the reference lists of the generation papers in the atlas, so it shows the theory that the generation literature draws on rather than the whole of gesture studies. Records were filled from the abstract (†) or from bibliographic metadata only (‡), and the theory label and area were assigned from the title and abstract. Corrections are welcome.

Areas: `psycholinguistics`, `linguistics`, `cognition`, `development`, `neuroscience`, `social-interaction`, `clinical`, `education`, `perception`, `other`. See [docs/SCHEMA.md](docs/SCHEMA.md).
