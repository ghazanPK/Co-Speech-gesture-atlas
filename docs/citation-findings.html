Author visibility: an exploratory proxy

For each publication, we count strictly earlier generation-research records for its listed authors and use the largest author count. Names are matched exactly, without identity disambiguation. The correlation is weak and varies across cohorts; it does not establish a general author-influence mechanism. Corresponding authors are not identified in the schema, and author order is not used to infer them.

Possible explanations and checks

Older publications have had more time to be cited. Reusable code, shared datasets, benchmark roles and exposure through venues may make publications easier to encounter or build on. Foundational papers may also be cited for historical context. These are plausible explanations to test, rather than mechanisms established by this graph.
